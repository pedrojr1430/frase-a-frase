## 20-reunioes
Let's get started. | Abertura padrão de reunião. Também: "Let's kick things off".
The first item on the agenda is the budget. | "Agenda" = pauta. "Item" = ponto da pauta.
Does anyone have any questions? | "Anyone" em perguntas; "someone" em afirmações.
I'd like to add something. | Educado para pedir a palavra.
Sorry to interrupt. | Também: "Sorry, can I jump in?".
Let's get back to the main topic. | "Get back to" = voltar a.
Can you share your screen? | Frase essencial em reuniões on-line.
You cut out. | "Cut out" = cortar (áudio, ligação).
You're on mute. | "Mute" = mudo. "Unmute" = tirar do mudo.
Let's leave it for the next meeting. | Também: "Let's table this" (EUA: adiar o assunto).
Who's taking notes today? | Ata formal: "minutes".
To sum up, we agreed on three points. | "To sum up" = resumindo.
We need to make a decision today. | Decisão se "make", nunca "take" em inglês americano.
I'll email you the minutes. | "Email" também é verbo: "I'll email you".
Can we postpone the meeting? | Também: "push the meeting back" (adiar).
We're running a little behind. | "Behind schedule" = atrasado em relação ao planejado.
Good question, I'll check and get back to you. | Resposta segura quando você não sabe algo.
What do you think of the proposal? | "Think of" = achar de, opinar sobre.
We need to define the next steps. | "Next steps" é expressão muito usada no trabalho.
Thank you all for your time. | Fechamento educado de reunião.
## 21-email-telefone
Dear Mr. Smith, | Abertura formal. Para mulheres sem saber o estado civil: "Ms.".
I'm writing regarding your order. | "Regarding" = a respeito de.
Please find the contract attached. | Também: "I've attached the contract".
I look forward to hearing from you. | "Look forward to" + verbo com "-ing".
Best regards, | Fechamento formal. Mais simples: "Best," ou "Thanks,".
Sorry for the late reply. | Também: "Apologies for the delay".
Hello? | Ao atender o telefone. Em empresas: "Hello, this is Ana speaking".
May I ask who's calling? | Forma educada de perguntar quem fala.
One moment, I'll put you through. | "Put through" = transferir a ligação.
He's not available right now. Can I take a message? | "Take a message" = anotar recado.
I think you have the wrong number. | Frase padrão para ligação errada.
I'll call back later. | "Call back" = retornar a ligação.
I can't hear you well, the signal is bad. | Também: "You're breaking up" (sua voz está cortando).
Could you spell your last name? | "Last name" ou "surname" = sobrenome.
I confirm receipt of the document. | Formal. Mais simples: "I got the document, thanks".
I'm copying my colleague. | "Cc" vem de "carbon copy". Também: "I've cc'd my colleague".
I'm forwarding yesterday's email. | "Forward" = encaminhar.
I'd appreciate a reply by Friday. | "By" + dia = até.
If you have any questions, feel free to reach out. | "Reach out" = entrar em contato.
I'll text you when I leave. | "Text" = mandar mensagem de celular.
## 22-atendimento
How can I help you? | Também: "What can I do for you?".
I'll be right with you. | Dito quando o atendente ainda está ocupado.
Sorry for the inconvenience. | "Inconvenience" = transtorno.
I'd like to make a complaint. | "Complaint" = reclamação. Verbo: "complain".
Could you give me your order number? | "Order number" = número do pedido.
I'll look into it right away. | "Look into" = verificar, investigar.
We'll refund your money. | "Refund" = reembolso.
We'll fix it within 24 hours. | "Within" = dentro de (um prazo).
I understand your situation. | Frase de empatia no atendimento.
I'll transfer you to the right department. | "Department" = setor, departamento.
Is there anything else I can help you with? | Fechamento padrão de atendimento.
The product arrived damaged. | "Damaged" = danificado.
I still haven't received my order. | "Still" + negativa = ainda não.
We'll send you a new one free of charge. | "Free of charge" = sem custo.
Could you fill out this form? | Nos EUA: "fill out". No Reino Unido: "fill in".
Your feedback is very important to us. | "Feedback" não tem plural.
I'm sorry about what happened. | Desculpa sincera, mais pessoal.
Our business hours are nine to six. | "Business hours" = horário de funcionamento.
We serve customers on a first-come, first-served basis. | Expressão fixa: quem chega primeiro é atendido primeiro.
Thank you for your patience. | "Patience" = paciência.
## 23-vendas
What's your best price? | Pergunta típica de negociação.
I can give you a ten percent discount. | "Percent" em uma palavra nos EUA.
The price doesn't include tax. | Nos EUA o imposto ("sales tax") é somado no caixa.
That's our final offer. | Também: "That's as low as we can go".
I need to check with my partner. | "Business partner" = sócio.
What payment terms do you offer? | "Payment terms" = condições de pagamento.
If you buy more units, the price goes down. | Também: "We offer volume discounts".
I'll send you a quote tomorrow. | "Quote" = orçamento (de preço).
I'm sure we can reach an agreement. | "Reach an agreement" = chegar a um acordo.
That's out of our budget. | "Budget" = orçamento disponível.
When could you deliver? | "Deliver" = entregar.
Shipping is free on orders over a hundred dollars. | "Shipping" = frete.
Let me suggest an alternative. | "Suggest" + substantivo, sem "to you".
It's a top-quality product. | "Top-quality" = de primeira linha.
We'll sign the contract next week. | "Sign" se pronuncia "sáin": o "g" é mudo.
Does it come with a warranty? | "Warranty" ou "guarantee" = garantia.
We need to close the deal this month. | "Close the deal" = fechar a venda.
It's an investment, not an expense. | Argumento clássico de vendas.
What's holding you back? | "Hold back" = segurar, deixar em dúvida.
It's a deal. | Também: "Deal!".
## 24-financas
Profits went up this quarter. | "Quarter" = trimestre.
We have to cut costs. | "Cut costs" = reduzir despesas.
The invoice is due on the thirtieth. | "Due" = com vencimento.
We need to issue the invoice. | "Issue" = emitir.
What's the margin on this product? | "Profit margin" = margem de lucro.
Sales dropped five percent. | "Drop" = cair. Também: "fall", "decline".
We're within budget. | O contrário: "over budget".
I have to file my tax return. | "Tax return" = declaração de imposto de renda.
The company is in the red. | No vermelho = "in the red". No azul = "in the black".
Inflation is still high. | "Still" = ainda.
We received the payment. | "Payment" = pagamento.
The client still hasn't paid. | "Client" para serviços; "customer" para lojas.
We need to go over the numbers. | "Go over" = revisar.
The annual results are positive. | "Annual" = anual.
We're looking for investors. | "Investor" = investidor.
Stocks went down. | Também: "shares". "Stock market" = bolsa de valores.
Salaries are paid at the end of the month. | Nos EUA é comum receber a cada duas semanas.
We're going to apply for a loan. | "Credit line" = linha de crédito.
Interest rates are very high. | "Interest" = juros.
We need to keep an eye on cash flow. | "Keep an eye on" = ficar de olho.
## 25-comida
How do you make apple pie? | Torta de maçã é um clássico dos EUA e do Reino Unido.
Let it simmer over low heat. | "Simmer" = cozinhar em fogo baixo, quase fervendo.
Peel and chop the potatoes. | "Peel" = descascar. "Chop" = picar.
Add a pinch of salt. | "A pinch" = uma pitada.
Fish and chips is a typical British dish. | "Chips" no Reino Unido são batatas fritas; nos EUA são "fries".
I love burgers. | "Burger" é a forma curta de "hamburger".
Clam chowder is made with clams and cream. | Sopa típica da Nova Inglaterra (EUA).
Can you send me the recipe? | "Recipe" se pronuncia "RÉ-ci-pi".
It needs a little more salt. | Também: "It's a bit bland" (sem gosto).
This is way too salty. | "Way too" = demais, bem informal.
Preheat the oven to 350 degrees. | Nos EUA o forno é em Fahrenheit: 350 °F ≈ 180 °C.
Pies are baked in the oven. | "Bake" = assar no forno (pães, bolos, tortas).
I'm making pancakes today. | "Pancakes" são panquecas doces, típicas do café da manhã.
Do you like spicy food? | "Spicy" = apimentado.
Mix all the ingredients well. | "Mix" = misturar.
I burned the rice. | Britânico: "burnt".
The avocado is ripe. | "Ripe" = maduro.
For breakfast I have toast with eggs. | "Have" é o verbo comum para comer e beber.
Today is home-cooked meal day. | "Home-cooked" = feito em casa.
Serve chilled. | "Chilled" = bem gelado.
## 26-esportes
Which team do you support? | Nos EUA: "Which team do you root for?".
I'm a Lakers fan. | "Fan" = torcedor.
Goal! | No futebol americano, o ponto principal é "touchdown".
The game ended in a tie. | Britânico: "The match ended in a draw".
We won two to one. | Também: "We won 2-1".
The referee gave a penalty. | "Referee" ou "ref" = juiz.
I play tennis on Saturdays. | Esportes com bola levam "play".
I go to the gym after work. | "Gym" = academia.
I run five kilometers every morning. | Nos EUA muitos usam milhas: 3 milhas ≈ 5 km.
Is the game on TV? | "On TV" = passando na TV.
It's the championship final. | "Final" = final. Nos EUA: "the finals".
I signed up for swimming lessons. | "Sign up for" = se inscrever em.
My team got relegated. | "Relegated" = rebaixado (futebol europeu).
It was offside. | "Offside" = impedimento.
The goalkeeper saved the penalty. | "Goalie" é a forma informal.
Practice is at seven today. | Nos EUA: "practice". No Reino Unido: "training".
I hurt my knee playing soccer. | Nos EUA: "soccer". No Reino Unido: "football".
She's the league's top scorer. | "Top scorer" = artilheira.
We're going to the stadium on Sunday. | "Stadium" = estádio.
We need more practice. | "Practice makes perfect" = a prática leva à perfeição.
## 27-tecnologia
My computer froze. | "Freeze" = travar. Passado: "froze".
Have you tried restarting it? | Também: "turning it off and on again".
My battery died. | "Die" = acabar (bateria). Também: "My phone is dead".
Download the app. | "Download" = baixar. "Upload" = subir, enviar.
I'm not getting any signal. | Também: "I have no bars".
I forgot my password. | "Reset your password" = redefinir a senha.
I need to update my phone. | "Update" = atualizar.
I'll send it to you on WhatsApp. | "On" para aplicativos e redes sociais.
Make a backup. | Também: "Back it up".
Artificial intelligence is changing the way we work. | "AI" = inteligência artificial.
This page won't load. | "Won't" mostra que algo se recusa a funcionar.
What's your username? | "Username" = nome de usuário.
Share the link in the group. | "Share" = compartilhar.
The screen is cracked. | "Cracked" = trincado.
I need to delete some files, my storage is full. | "Storage" = armazenamento.
My account got hacked. | "Got hacked" = foi hackeada.
Upload the photos to the cloud. | "The cloud" = a nuvem.
The keyboard isn't working. | "Keyboard" = teclado.
He spends way too much time on social media. | "Social media" é incontável.

We need to recycle plastic. | "Recycle" = reciclar.

I sort my trash at home. | "Sort" = separar.

## 29-cinema-musica
What's playing at the movies? | Britânico: "What's on at the cinema?".
I prefer watching it in the original language. | "Dubbed" = dublado.
Does it have subtitles? | "Subtitles" = legendas. "Captions" = legendas para surdos.
I thought the movie was slow. | "Movie" (EUA) e "film" (Reino Unido).
Have you seen that director's latest series? | "Series" é igual no singular e no plural.
In the end, the main character dies. | "In the end" = no final.
No spoilers! | "Spoiler" veio do inglês "spoil" (estragar).
Two tickets for the eight o'clock show. | "Showing" ou "show" = sessão.
I love pop music. | Gêneros musicais sem artigo: "I love jazz".
What kind of music do you listen to? | "Listen to" sempre com "to".
I've played the guitar since I was a kid. | "Play the guitar" com "the" para instrumentos.
The concert was amazing. | "Gig" = show, de forma informal.
This song is really popular right now. | "Trending" também se usa.
I always sing in the shower. | "In the shower" = no chuveiro.
Jazz was born in New Orleans. | O jazz surgiu nos EUA no início do século XX.
It won the Oscar for best picture. | "Best picture" = melhor filme.
It's a really funny comedy. | "Funny" = engraçado. "Fun" = divertido.
The book is better than the movie. | Comparativo: "better than".
We're going to a music festival this summer. | "Festival" = festival.
Can you recommend a good show? | "Show" nos EUA também significa série de TV.
## 30-historia-sociedade
The United States declared independence in 1776. | Datas: "in" + ano.
Today is a national holiday. | "Holiday" = feriado.
The elections are next Sunday. | "Election" = eleição.
The Romans built Hadrian's Wall in Britain. | Muralha de Adriano, no norte da Inglaterra.
English is spoken in more than fifty countries. | Voz passiva: "is spoken".
Inequality is still a problem. | "Inequality" = desigualdade.
Youth unemployment is very high. | "Youth" = juventude, dos jovens.
More and more people live in cities. | "More and more" = cada vez mais.
Stonehenge was built thousands of years ago. | "Ago" = atrás, há (tempo).
Housing prices keep going up. | "Keep" + "-ing" = continuar fazendo.
Thanksgiving is an American tradition. | O Dia de Ação de Graças é na 4ª quinta de novembro.
The protest went through downtown. | "Protest" = manifestação.
The population is aging. | Britânico: "ageing".
Today's news is worrying. | "News" é singular: "the news is".

Martin Luther King Jr. fought for civil rights. | "Civil rights" = direitos civis.
It's a very controversial topic. | "Controversial" = polêmico.
Public education is free. | "Free" = gratuito e também livre.
